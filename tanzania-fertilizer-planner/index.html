Method & assumptions

Read this before quoting a number. The field layer is a modelled cropping surface, not a field survey: a 0.055° grid (≈37 km² per cell) is scored for rainfall, floodplain and coastal position, market access and protected-area status, then cropped area is allocated to the crops that actually dominate each agro-ecological zone here — cane and paddy in the Kilombero and Rufiji floodplains, cashew and cassava on the coastal sands, sisal along the Tanga–Korogwe corridor, sorghum and sunflower on the Dodoma-side drylands.

Nutrient demand per hectare uses Tanzanian blanket recommendations by crop (N–P2O5–K2O), scaled by the two adoption sliders. Products are then allocated from nutrients at label analysis — DAP 18-46-0, Urea 46-0-0, MOP 0-0-60 and so on.

Swap the grid for your own shapefiles — a district crop census, NDVI-classified plots, or your dealer sell-out by ward — and every number downstream, territory KPIs included, recalculates unchanged.
